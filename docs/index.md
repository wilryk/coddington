# heliostat

Heliostat-field simulation for concentrating solar power towers: field
layouts, Monte Carlo ray tracing, flux maps, shading and blocking, DNI
handling, and annual energy.

This site is under construction alongside the v0.1.0 release. The planned
structure:

- **Getting started** — install, five-minute quick start (generate a field,
  trace a day, plot the flux map).
- **User guide** — fields and layouts, the heliostat model, receivers and
  secondaries, DNI and site setup, annual energy, the run-store format.
- **Physics reference** — the trace chain, sunshape models, shading and
  blocking math, the efficiency-interpolation method.
- **Examples** and **API reference**.
